# Headlines and quality

## Before scoring

The headline comes after audience, interest, goal, strategy, tactic and topic. In a new plan, it guides the content that will still be developed and verified. In a narrow review, the existing text limits what the headline can promise.

Create four alternatives with different reasoning. Don't force the same formula (number, question, contrast, brand) on every piece, or a fixed word limit: check readability in the real composition.

## The ten criteria

| Key | Criterion | Question |
|---|---|---|
| right_audience | Right audience | Does the chosen person have a reason to stop? |
| relevance | Relevance | Does the discovery matter for what they want to decide, achieve or avoid? |
| clarity | Immediate clarity | Can it be understood without knowing the context or an obscure tool? |
| curiosity | Concrete curiosity | Does it open a specific question that the content answers? |
| payoff | Payoff | Does it show what the person gains by consuming it? |
| specificity | Specificity | Does it name a situation, mechanism or detail that helps understanding? |
| information_strength | Strength of the information | Is the subject interesting beyond the way it's written? |
| faithfulness | Faithfulness | Do the content and the sources support exactly the promise? |
| mobile_reading | Mobile reading | Does it fit and read well on the piece? |
| naturalness | Naturalness | Does it sound like something a person would say or share? |

Scale: 1 fails · 2 weak · 3 fair · 4 strong · 5 excellent. Average = total ÷ 10.

## Selection rule

Average ≥ 4 **and** a score ≥ 4 in right audience, relevance, clarity, curiosity, payoff and faithfulness. Weak payoff, curiosity or clarity are not compensated by the other scores. If no alternative passes, revise the topic or leave the piece pending.

Faithfulness is only confirmed after the research. During ideation it is provisional: record what needs to be verified. Without proof, don't claim numbers, profit, customers, results or causes.

## Traps

- A sentence that's curious for a consumer can be irrelevant for a business owner.
- A famous name can be clear and still hide the reason to watch.
- If the headline needs a long caption to reveal the subject, it needs to be revised.
- If the topic isn't interesting, no headline fixes it.

## How to record

For each piece: the four alternatives, the ten scores with justification, the average, the biggest weakness, the chosen one (it can stay empty) and the passage that supports the promise. These evaluations are internal and never go into the art or the caption.

When you change a headline, update the title, cover, opening, first art text, calendar and script together.
