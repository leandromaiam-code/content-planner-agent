# Strategy, CTAs and measurement

## Brand moment

- **Foundation:** make the proposition understandable and show a path forward. Useful functions: show what is possible, make the problem recognizable, explain why it matters, reveal the path to the result, present the approach, explain the difference, demonstrate the transformation, answer doubts with proof and show how to start. If the foundation already exists, don't start over from it.
- **Discovery:** grow among the right people. Prioritize subjects that interest people who don't know you yet, with a clear payoff and a reason to be shared. Growing is not any view at all.
- **Acquisition:** combine attention, interest, desire and conversion according to the buying cycle and the offer. Any ratio (e.g. 7 attention, 3 interest, 3 desire, 1 conversion every 14 posts) is a hypothesis to test, not a rule.
- **Launch:** a variant with its own stages (anticipation, sign-up capture, warm-up, event, cart opening). Every capture post asks for the same action, the sign-up, with different arguments. Don't use absolute dates before the event date exists.

## Strategy × tactic

**Strategy:** who to prioritize, what change to seek, which messages and mix of functions to use, the approach and the role of the CTAs.
**Tactic:** format, composition, template, trend, language and execution.
**Topic:** the narrowed subject of each piece.

No ratio is filled with vague phrases: calculate quantities, totals and frequency. Don't confuse promotion budget with production cost.

## CTA and result

| Goal | Expected behavior | CTA (when the piece supports it) | How to measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grow | Qualified strangers discover you and want to follow | Follow for a clear editorial promise; send to someone | Reach among non-followers, profile visits, new followers per reach |
| Engage | The audience participates, sees value and comes back | Save something to consult later; comment on a decision; send an example | Retention, saves, shares, relevant comments |
| Convert | The person takes the next commercial step | Ask for a diagnosis, try or buy, with a real destination | Qualified conversations, leads, sales, conversion rate |

One main action per piece. Don't ask people to "like, comment, save, share and book" at the same time. A CTA doesn't save an irrelevant topic. Offering material ("comment X and I'll send it to you") requires the material to exist and someone to deliver it.

## Subject territories

Use them only when they serve the audience, never to fill the calendar:

| Territory | Example subjects |
|---|---|
| New business models | One-person businesses, subscriptions, a service that becomes a product |
| Behind the scenes | What supports a simple experience for the customer |
| New ways to sell | Selling through conversation, creators, affiliates, agents |
| Niche opportunities | A sector's needs and new ways to meet them |
| Growth and distribution | First customers, partnerships, referrals, community |
| Brands that become desire | Positioning, experience and belonging |
| Changes in buying behavior | New consumer expectations and decisions |
| Real experiments | Observable execution, with results and limits |
| Founder decisions | Choosing opportunities, testing bets, allocating time and money |

## History and learning

Read previous topics, headlines, formats, openings, CTAs and metrics, with dates. Record the hypothesis of each test and change one main thing at a time so you can compare. Compare equivalent windows and bases. A single post doesn't prove cause, and missing data doesn't allow you to invent a "winning format".
